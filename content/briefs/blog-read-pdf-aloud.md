## Key findings

- **Edge is the best free option for Windows**: right-click any PDF > Read Aloud, no extension, uses Immersive Reader
- **Chrome has no built-in TTS for PDFs** — needs extension (Natural Reader, Read&Write)
- **macOS**: Preview > Edit > Speech; VoiceOver for hands-free
- **iPhone**: Accessibility > Spoken Content > Speak Screen + two-finger swipe
- **Android**: Select to Speak accessibility feature; or Google Play Books
- **Scanned PDFs** have no text layer, so TTS produces nothing or garbage — OCR needed first
  - Google Drive's built-in OCR is the free fix (open PDF with Google Docs)
- **Large PDFs** can be slow to load; compressing image-heavy files first helps load time
