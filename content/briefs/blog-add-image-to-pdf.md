## Two core use cases (very different!)

1. **Image as new page** - most common for job apps, portfolios, reports: convert image to PDF, merge in.
2. **Image overlaid on existing page** - logo, photo, stamp: needs a browser editor that supports image XObject placement.

## Our tools' honest capability

- `jpg-to-pdf`: converts JPG/PNG to a one-page-per-image PDF
- `merge-pdf`: merges PDFs; measured output = 970.5 KB for 971.9 KB inputs (no inflation)
- `sign-pdf`: for signatures specifically
- `organize-pdf`: reorder pages after merging
- `edit-pdf`: does NOT support images (text and rectangles only) — do not recommend for this
