## Competitor gaps

- Most articles push Adobe Acrobat Pro or paid software, without mentioning free browser-based options.
- Few explain the two different meanings of "batch convert" (all to one PDF vs each to its own PDF).
- Most don't address the order/reorder step, which people care about.
- Privacy angle largely ignored: most tools upload files to a server.
