# Research Brief: how to make a scanned pdf searchable free

**Topic ID:** blog-make-searchable
**Target query:** how to make a scanned pdf searchable free
**Secondary keywords:** ocr pdf free, searchable pdf, make pdf searchable, ocr scanned document free, add text layer to pdf
**Intent:** informational
**Surface:** blog
**Slug:** how-to-make-a-scanned-pdf-searchable-free

## Search intent

Reader has a scanned PDF (photographed or flatbed-scanned document) and can't search text with Ctrl+F / Cmd+F. They want a free fix. Most won't know the term "OCR" — they just know the search doesn't work.

## Real questions (from PAA, Quora, Reddit)

1. Why can't I search text in my scanned PDF? (most readers don't know why)
2. What is OCR and how does it create a searchable PDF?
3. How do I make a scanned PDF searchable using Google Drive (free, no sign-up beyond Google account)
4. What free online tools can do OCR without installing software?
5. Can I make a PDF searchable offline for free (privacy-sensitive docs)?
6. How accurate is OCR and what makes it fail?
7. What can I do with the PDF once it's searchable?

## Competitor gaps

- Adobe's hub page focuses on their paid tools; free path is buried.
- Most posts don't explain WHY scanned PDFs aren't searchable (image vs. text).
- Few cover the privacy angle: uploading medical/legal docs to web services is a real concern.
- Almost none mention OCRmyPDF for free offline processing.
- Most don't warn about accuracy limitations (handwriting, low-res scans).

## Our angle

Honest, practical guide: explain the "why", then give three distinct free paths ranked by ease and privacy. We don't have an OCR tool, so we acknowledge that clearly and recommend the best free options. We link to our Merge PDF and Split PDF tools for natural pre/post-OCR workflows (splitting large files before OCR, merging searchable sections after).
