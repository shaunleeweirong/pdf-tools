## Built-in iPhone option (Files app)

Long-press a PDF > Quick Actions > Optimize File Size. No quality controls; does not work on all files; replaces the original. Good for a quick no-decisions fix, not reliable for files that are already compact or contain only text.

## Browser-based angle (our competitive win)

Our Compress PDF tool runs in Safari with no install, no account, no server upload. Gives three quality levels. Files stay in the browser. This beats the built-in option for control and the app-store options for zero friction.

## The critical warning (E-E-A-T differentiator)

Most competitor pages tell readers to just compress any PDF. None warn clearly:
- Image/scan PDFs: compress well. Desktop-measured: 913.4 KB image PDF shrank to 315.3 KB at Medium (-65.5%).
- Text PDFs: get dramatically bigger. Desktop-measured: 23.5 KB text report grew to 1.4 MB.
- Mixed (text + small images): also gets bigger. 35 KB grew to 171.2 KB at Medium.
- Never recommend compressing twice.

**Important caveat for post:** these numbers are from desktop testing (macOS, Chromium). Mobile behavior is not yet measured. Frame as "in desktop testing" and note Safari on iPhone may produce different exact numbers.
