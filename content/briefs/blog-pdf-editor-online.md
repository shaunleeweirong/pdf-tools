## Competitor angle and our gap

Most comparison posts list 8-10 tools without distinguishing:
- Browser-based (file never leaves your device) vs server-based (file uploaded)
- Annotating (adding text boxes on top) vs truly editing existing characters
- Which features actually stay free vs what gets gated

Gap we beat:
- We explain the fundamental why (PDF format stores text by position, not as a flow)
- We clarify the privacy distinction clearly
- We give honest guidance on when NOT to use compression (text-heavy PDFs get bigger)
- All our tools are free, no account, browser-based
