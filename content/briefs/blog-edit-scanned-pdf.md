## The two approaches to understand

**Overlay (no OCR):** Add new text boxes or annotations on top of the scanned image. The original scan stays unchanged; you're just drawing on top. Works in any PDF editor that supports annotation/text insertion. Fast, free, no accuracy risk. Limitation: you can't change the original scanned words, only cover them or add new content nearby.

**OCR (text extraction):** Run Optical Character Recognition to detect characters in the scanned image and build a text layer. The result has selectable, searchable, editable text. Free option: Google Docs (upload to Google Drive -> right-click -> Open with Google Docs). Accuracy depends on scan quality; low-quality scans or handwriting produce errors.
