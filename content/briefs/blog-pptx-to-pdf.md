3. LibreOffice Impress — free, offline, open-source

## Key facts to include (from tool-measurements.md)

- compress-pdf rasterizes pages to JPEG; great for image-heavy PDFs (913.4 KB deck → 315.3 KB at Medium, -65.5%)
- Do NOT recommend compressing text-only slides (23.5 KB text report → 1.4 MB after compression)
- merge-pdf does not inflate size meaningfully (971.9 KB in, 970.5 KB out)
