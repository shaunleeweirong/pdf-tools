## Competitor structure and gaps

Competitors (Smallpdf, Wondershare, Adobe) cover:
- Lists of causes (images, fonts, scans, hidden data)
- Generic advice: "use Acrobat" or "use an online compressor"

**Gaps we fill:**
- No competitor warns with data that compressing a text PDF makes it MUCH larger
- Our measured data: text-report.pdf (23.5 KB) → 1,400 KB at Medium compression (+5,861%)
- Our measured data: image deck (913.4 KB) → 315.3 KB at Medium (-65.5%)
- Concrete distinction: rasterization-based compression helps image/scan PDFs, hurts text PDFs
- Free, in-browser alternative: no sign-up, no upload to external servers
- Honest advice: "here's when NOT to use compression" (our differentiator)
