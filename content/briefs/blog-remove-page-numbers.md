## Key finding: three types of page numbers

1. **Text-based**: Regular text on the page, selectable and deletable with a PDF editor
2. **Header/footer-added**: Added by Word, Google Docs, or Acrobat - easiest to remove from the source file
3. **Scanned/rasterized**: Part of a photograph of the page, not real text, can only be covered
