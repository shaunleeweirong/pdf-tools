## Key facts from research
- Screen readers require: real selectable text, tags (structure), document title + language in metadata, alt text for images
- PDF/UA (ISO 14289) is the formal standard for accessible PDFs
- WCAG (W3C) provides broader accessibility principles that apply to PDFs
- Easiest free approach: well-structured Word/Google Docs export with accessibility settings
- Free fixer: PDFix.io auto-tags existing PDFs online, no sign-up
- Google Drive OCR can convert scanned PDFs to editable Docs for free
- Our edit-metadata tool sets document title/language - directly relevant
