## Search intent

Informational/transactional: The reader wants to stop others from copy-pasting text or sharing their PDF without permission. Some want to gate access entirely; others want to share openly but prevent content extraction.

## Real questions people ask (PAA / Reddit / Quora)

1. Does adding a password to a PDF actually stop copying?
2. Can I let people read my PDF but prevent them from copying the text?
3. What are PDF permissions/restrictions and do they work?
4. Is watermarking a PDF effective copy protection?
5. Can someone bypass PDF copy protection?
6. How can I protect a PDF from being copied without a password?
7. What is the most effective free method to prevent PDF copying?

## What competitors cover (and their gaps)

- Most articles push paid DRM tools (Locklizard, Adobe Rights Management) as the only real solution
- Free advice usually just says "add a password" without explaining what that does vs. doesn't do
- Nobody clearly separates the two distinct things people want: (a) access gating (password to open) vs. (b) content restrictions (permission flags)
- Our angle: honest about what each approach does, everything runs in the browser, no upload needed, two relevant free tools (protect-pdf and watermark-pdf)

## Recommended format

Blog post, 900 words, informational with how-to steps and quick comparison at the end.

Reader wants to prevent someone from copy-pasting their PDF content, or at minimum wants to know if what they're trying to do is even possible. Give them two free, no-upload options and be honest that no free method is truly copy-proof.
