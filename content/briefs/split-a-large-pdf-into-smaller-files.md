## Measured facts available (from docs/marketing/tool-measurements.md)

- Gmail and Outlook both cap at 25 MB; Base64 adds ~33%, so practical ceiling before attaching is ~18 MB.
- Compressing a 20-page text report grew it from 23.5 KB to 1.4 MB (compress = bad for text PDFs).
- Merging strips form fields (relevant for context — splitting preserves them per-page).
