## Key facts (all from docs/marketing/tool-measurements.md)
- Image deck (913.4 KB) → 315.3 KB at Medium quality (-65.5%)
- Text report (23.5 KB) → 1,400.8 KB at Medium (+5,861%) — DO NOT compress text PDFs
- Second compression pass: 315.3 KB → 598.9 KB (+90%) at same level — never chain passes
- Gmail and Outlook.com both cap at 25 MB; Base64 adds ~33%, so practical ceiling ~18 MB
