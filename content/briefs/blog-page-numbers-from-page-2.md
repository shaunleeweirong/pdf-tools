## Why people search this

Academic papers, business reports, legal filings, and book-style PDFs all follow a convention: the cover page (and often a table of contents) carries no visible page number, while the numbered body starts at page 1 or continues from a roman numeral section. Someone has a finished PDF and needs to apply that convention without Acrobat.

## Two distinct interpretations (most posts miss this)

1. **Skip display only** — page 1 gets no stamp; page 2 shows "2", page 3 shows "3". The total count is correct but the cover is blank.  
2. **Reset count** — page 1 gets no stamp; page 2 shows "1", page 3 shows "2". Used in formal reports where body numbering restarts at 1.

Most how-to posts conflate these. Covering the distinction is the main gap vs. competitors.
