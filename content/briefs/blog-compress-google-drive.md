## Why this topic

People bump into large PDFs eating their 15 GB Google Drive quota or slowing down shared-link loading. Most guides send users to tools that require uploading to third-party servers. Our in-browser tool is the privacy answer. But the critical gap competitors miss: they don't tell users that compression only helps image-heavy or scanned PDFs, not text PDFs (which get dramatically bigger).

## Google Drive constraints (verified)

- Individual file upload limit: 5 TB (rarely an issue for PDFs)
- Free storage quota: 15 GB shared across Drive, Gmail, and Photos
- 750 GB daily upload limit
- Source: filesize.org/limits/google-drive, Google Workspace admin docs
