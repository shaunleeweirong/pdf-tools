## The E-E-A-T differentiator (measurements)

Our compressor rasterizes every page to JPEG. That means:

- Image-heavy PDF (913.4 KB, 6 pages): High → 175.6 KB (-80.8%), Medium → 315.3 KB (-65.5%), Low → 703.5 KB (-23.0%)
- Text-only report (23.5 KB, 20 pages): Medium → 1,400.8 KB (+5,861%). DO NOT compress text PDFs.
- Re-compressing (315.3 KB Medium output): second Medium pass → 598.9 KB (+90%)

These measured results are in docs/marketing/tool-measurements.md and are the core of the post.
