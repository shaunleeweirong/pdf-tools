## Competitor analysis

- Smallpdf, Adobe, PDF24 all lead with "use our tool" (upload-based). They downplay the privacy concern.
- Most posts ignore the built-in methods (Word's own Save As PDF, LibreOffice, Mac print-to-PDF).
- Font substitution as the root cause of formatting problems is mentioned but not well explained.
- Almost no post addresses the real risk: uploading a sensitive document to a server.

## Our angle / gap

We don't have a Word-to-PDF converter. That's fine. Our angle is:
- Lead with the best-quality method (Word's built-in export), explain WHY it works better
- Address the privacy concern head-on (built-in methods never upload your file)
- Honestly explain what causes formatting breaks (font substitution)
- After conversion, link to our PDF tools: Merge PDF, Protect PDF, Compress PDF (with caveats from measurements)

## Key facts (E-E-A-T anchors)

- Font substitution is the #1 cause of formatting issues — Word's own export uses installed fonts and avoids this
- Google Docs: upload .docx, File > Download > PDF — free with Google account
- LibreOffice: free, open-source, runs on device — no upload
- Mac: Print > PDF > Save as PDF — works from any app
- Our Compress PDF: works well on image-heavy PDFs; text PDFs get larger (23.5 KB → 1.4 MB per measurements). Never recommend compressing a text document.
- Our Merge PDF: good for combining separately-converted chapters
- Our Protect PDF: AES-256, in-browser, file never leaves device
