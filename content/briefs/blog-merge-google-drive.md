## Key finding

Google Drive has NO built-in PDF merge feature. This surprises most searchers. The answer must lead with this clearly, then give the fastest workaround.

## Real questions (PAA / Reddit / Quora mining)

- Can Google Drive merge PDFs natively?
- How do I combine PDFs in Google Drive without downloading them?
- What is the best free Google Drive PDF merge add-on?
- How do I merge PDFs stored in Google Drive on a phone?
- Will merging PDFs in Google Drive affect quality or file size?
- Do Google Workspace Marketplace add-ons for PDF merging cost money?
- What happens to form fields when I merge PDFs?

## Competitor angles + gaps

Top competitors (pdfgear.com, updf.com, wondershare, swifdoo) cover:
- "Use a third-party add-on" as the main answer
- Add-on installation steps (PDF Merge and Split, Merge PDF by Jotform)
- Download-merge-reupload as alternative

Gap we beat:
- Privacy angle: none of the competitors clearly explain the trade-off of giving Workspace add-ons access to your Drive files
- Specific, measured file-size numbers (they all say "file size won't change much" without real data)
- The form-fields warning: most pages skip this entirely
