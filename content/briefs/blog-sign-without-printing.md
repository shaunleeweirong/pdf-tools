## Competitor angles + our gap

Competitors (Signaturely, SignWell, SigPDF) cover the topic broadly but:
- Push their paid SaaS e-sign products as the primary answer
- Understate the built-in Mac/iPhone options most people already have
- Don't emphasize in-browser/no-upload privacy angle

**Our angle:** fastest free option (browser tool, no upload, no account) + clear per-platform steps for the tools people already have.
