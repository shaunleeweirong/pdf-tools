## Use-case context (from research)

The 500KB limit appears heavily on:
- Indian government exam portals (UPSC, SSC, NTA)
- Visa and immigration applications
- HR portals and job boards
- University admissions forms
- Institutional submission systems

People are typically trying to submit a resume, scanned certificate, ID document, or form that was rejected for being over the limit.

## Key findings from competitor pages

Competitors (smallseotools, duplichecker, wondershare, adobe) focus on:
- Upload + compress + download flow description
- Marketing language about "quality preserved"
- No distinction between PDF types (text vs image)

**Gap we can exploit:** None of the competitors explain that text-heavy PDFs will NOT shrink and will actually get larger. Our post is honest about this, backed by real measurements. This is the single most useful thing we can tell the reader.

## E-E-A-T grounding (all from tool-measurements.md)

- photo-deck.pdf (913.4 KB) at Medium: 315.3 KB (-65.5%)
- photo-deck.pdf at High: 175.6 KB (-80.8%)
- text-report.pdf (23.5 KB) at Medium: 1400.8 KB (+5861%) — DO NOT compress text PDFs
- Second Medium pass on already-compressed file: 315.3 KB -> 598.9 KB (+90%)
- Second High pass on already-compressed file: 315.3 KB -> 297.8 KB (-5.6%) — barely worth it

**Caveat:** Test corpus uses illustrated images, not real photographs. Post should describe results as from an "image-heavy PDF" and note real results vary by content type.
