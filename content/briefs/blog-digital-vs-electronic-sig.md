## Competitor gaps

Competitor articles (Smallpdf, iLovePDF, Entrust) give the definition but tend to stop at "digital is more secure." They don't clearly tell the reader which one to use for their actual situation, and they don't explain that "digital signature" is often used colloquially to mean either thing.

## Gap we beat

Be direct: our Sign PDF tool creates an electronic signature (not a PKI digital signature). Say so, be honest, and explain what that means for the reader. Competitors often imply their tools do more than they do. Also: address that most people saying "I need a digital signature" actually just mean an electronic signature.
