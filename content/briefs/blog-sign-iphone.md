## Competitor angles + our gap

Competitors (Smallpdf, PDFGear, PDFFiller blogs):
- Nearly all push their own apps requiring account creation or paid subscription
- Most mention Markup but bury it under app promos
- Few address the "Markup icon is greyed out / PDF opens in read-only viewer" pain point
- None clearly explain the limitation that browser tools may only sign the first page

Our advantage:
- Lead with the built-in free method (Markup, already on every iPhone)
- Cover the browser-based Sign PDF tool as a no-install alternative
- Be honest: browser tool signs first page only; Markup lets you navigate to any page
- Privacy: our browser tool doesn't upload files to a server
