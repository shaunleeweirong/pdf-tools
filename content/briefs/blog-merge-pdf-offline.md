## Reader's job-to-be-done

They have 2 to 5 PDF files they need as one: a cover letter + resume, a multi-part form, a set of scanned pages. They're on a plane, on a restricted network, or worried about uploading sensitive docs to a random website. They want the job done fast and privately.

## Key angles and what competitors miss

- Competitors focus on downloadable desktop apps (PDFsam, Adobe). Most miss the "in-browser = no upload" angle that lets privacy-conscious readers use an online tool without sending files to a server.
- The **Mac Preview** path is genuinely free and offline; spell it out step-by-step.
- **Windows has no built-in merge** — be honest; readers need a free download OR an in-browser tool.
- Merging does NOT inflate file size (measured: 971.9 KB in, 970.5 KB out). Say so to reassure readers worried about email limits.
- **Filled forms lose their fields when merged.** Flatten first. Most competitor posts skip this.
