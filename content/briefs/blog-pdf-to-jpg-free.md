## Competitor gaps

Smallpdf, iLovePDF, pdf24, and Adobe all cover basic steps but:
- Most require upload to their servers
- Many impose watermarks or page limits on the free tier
- Few explain WHEN to convert vs keep as PDF
- Few explain quality tradeoffs honestly (no false "lossless" claims)

## Our angle

- Runs in-browser: no upload, no account, no watermark
- Honest quality guidance (nothing claimed that is not in tool-measurements.md)
- Explains use cases: social media, slides, email body, archiving scans
