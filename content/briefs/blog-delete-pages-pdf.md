# Research brief: how to delete pages from a pdf free

**targetQuery**: how to delete pages from a pdf free  
**Secondary keywords**: remove pages from pdf, delete pdf pages online, delete blank pages from pdf, remove pdf pages without acrobat  
**Search intent**: Informational — user wants to complete a specific task (remove one or more pages) using a free method  
**Surface**: blog

## Competitor angles + gaps

- Smallpdf, iLovePDF, Adobe, Sejda all rank for this query
- Most focus on their own tool's steps; few explain the privacy angle
- None strongly differentiate between server-side vs. browser-side processing (our edge)
- Mac Preview is mentioned by many but not well explained
- The "extract instead of delete" tip (useful when keeping fewer pages than removing) is absent on most pages
