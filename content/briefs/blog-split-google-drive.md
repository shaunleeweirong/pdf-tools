## The "get it done" angle

You have a PDF in Google Drive and just need to split it. The thing stopping you is that Google Drive has no native split feature. The post resolves that confusion quickly and gives one clear method that works with no add-on, no sign-up, and no file upload to a third-party server.

## Competitor angles and the gap we beat

- Competitors push Google Workspace Marketplace add-ons. Every add-on requires installation, account creation, and granting the app access to read/write Google Drive files. Many limit free use to a few files per day.
- Some competitors have server-upload models that send the file to their infrastructure — a concern for personal or confidential PDFs.
- Our gap: no installation, no sign-up, file stays in the browser, completely free. The download-split-re-upload flow takes about a minute.
