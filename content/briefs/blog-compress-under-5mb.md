## Key distinction the post must make

Our compressor rasterizes to JPEG. It helps image-heavy PDFs enormously and hurts text-heavy ones (makes them bigger). The post must explain this clearly before recommending the compressor.

Measured facts to use:
- Image-heavy 913.4 KB PDF → 175.6 KB at High (−80.8%)
- Same file → 315.3 KB at Medium (−65.5%)
- Same file → 703.5 KB at Low (−23.0%)
- Text-only 23.5 KB PDF → 1.4 MB at Medium (compression makes it 60x bigger)
- Second compression pass at Medium: 315.3 KB → 598.9 KB (+90%) — never chain passes

## Competitor gaps
