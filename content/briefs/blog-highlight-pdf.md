## Key findings from research

- Chrome 104+ and Microsoft Edge both have built-in PDF annotation (highlight in yellow or multiple colors) with no download or sign-up required.
- Mac Preview supports multi-color highlighting and is free on every Mac.
- Adobe Acrobat Reader (not Acrobat Pro) is a free desktop download and supports highlighting.
- The most common "why can't I highlight" reasons: (a) the PDF is a scanned image - no real text exists; (b) the PDF owner set permissions restricting annotation.
- Scanned PDFs need OCR (optical character recognition) before you can highlight text in them - we should be honest we don't have an OCR tool but can mention free alternatives.
- Locked/restricted PDFs need the owner password removed first - our /unlock-pdf tool is relevant here.
