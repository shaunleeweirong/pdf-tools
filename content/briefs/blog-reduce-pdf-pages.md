# Research Brief: How to Reduce the Number of Pages in a PDF Free

**targetQuery:** how to reduce the number of pages in a pdf free
**Secondary keywords:** remove pages from pdf, delete pdf pages free, extract pdf pages, pdf fewer pages, reduce pdf page count
**Search intent:** Informational/transactional — reader wants to end up with a shorter PDF document, or to print on fewer sheets
**Priority:** 2
**Surface:** Blog

## The gap competitors miss

Smallpdf, iLovePDF, Sejda, and Adobe all focus on one thing: delete pages from a PDF. They mostly ignore the second common meaning — "I need this 13-page document to print on fewer sheets of paper." A Quora thread specifically about this came up near the top of results. Covering both meanings in one clear post is the gap to fill.

Also: most competitors require sign-up or upload files to their servers. Our angle is free, no sign-up, files stay in the browser.
