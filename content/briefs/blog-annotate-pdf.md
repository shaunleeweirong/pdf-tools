# Research Brief: How to Annotate a PDF Free Online

**targetQuery:** how to annotate a pdf free online
**Secondary keywords:** annotate pdf without signing up, add comments to pdf free, annotate pdf in browser, draw on pdf online free, add text to pdf free online
**Search intent:** Informational — reader wants to mark up a PDF right now, probably for work or study, and wants free options that don't require an account or download.
**Surface:** Blog

## Real questions people ask

From PAA, Quora, and Reddit searches:

1. How do I annotate a PDF for free without creating an account?
2. What does "annotating a PDF" actually mean? What types of annotation are there?
3. How do I highlight text in a PDF for free online?
4. How do I add comments or sticky notes to a PDF for free?
5. How do I add text boxes or labels to a PDF (not just highlight)?
6. How do I draw on a PDF for free?
7. How do I save annotations so they don't disappear when I close the file?
8. Why can't I annotate my PDF? (greyed-out tools, locked PDF, scanned PDF)
9. Do my files get uploaded when I use an online PDF annotator?

## Key differentiators / gaps in competitor content

- Most competitor posts lead with sign-up tools (Smallpdf, iLovePDF, LightPDF) and bury the browser-native options.
- Browser-native (Chrome/Edge) and our own in-browser Edit PDF tool require zero upload and zero account — that's the privacy/convenience angle competitors gloss over.
- The "annotations disappearing" problem (why to flatten) is almost never explained.
- The scanned PDF edge case (no text layer, OCR needed) is important and underserved.

## Internal link targets

- `/edit-pdf` — for adding text boxes and labels anywhere on a page
- `/flatten-pdf` — for locking annotations as permanent page content before sharing

## Outbound authority links

- Microsoft Edge PDF annotation docs (official support.microsoft.com)
- Google Drive OCR help (official support.google.com)

## Recommended format

Blog post, how-to with multiple H2 sections per annotation type. ~1000 words. No separate FAQ body section (FAQ goes in frontmatter). Primary CTA: Edit PDF tool for text boxes; Flatten PDF for making annotations permanent.

## "Get it done" angle

Reader is trying to mark up a PDF right now — for a research paper, a contract review, a job application, or school notes — and doesn't want to sign up for yet another service. Lead with the fastest zero-friction option (browser), then cover each annotation type they might need.
