## Competitor gaps we beat

- Most competitors (Smallpdf, PDF24, ilovepdf) require file upload to their servers.
- Adobe requires sign-in or paid subscription.
- Privacy is not prominently explained on most competitor pages.
- Our angle: files never leave the browser (client-side via pdf-lib/WebAssembly) = privacy win for confidential documents (contracts, medical files, legal docs).
