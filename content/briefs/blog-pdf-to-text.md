## The big distinction competitors miss

Most ranked pages jump straight to listing tools. They skip the most important thing: there are two completely different problems here.

1. **Native PDF** (text is stored as vector glyphs): you can select text in the file. Copy-paste works. No OCR needed.
2. **Scanned PDF** (pages are raster images): text is just pixels. You must run OCR to get machine-readable text.

Until a reader understands which type they have, listing tools is useless.
