# Brief: why won't my pdf attach to email

**targetQuery:** why won't my pdf attach to email
**Secondary keywords:** pdf too large to email, email attachment size limit, pdf won't send, pdf file too big for email
**Search intent:** problem-solving / informational — reader has a PDF they're trying to email and it's failing; they want to know why and fix it fast
**Surface:** blog
**Recommended format:** problem → causes → solutions how-to → FAQ (~900-1000 words)

## Questions to answer (from research)

1. Why won't my PDF attach to email? (the umbrella question — multiple causes)
2. What are the attachment size limits for Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo?
3. Why does a 19MB PDF go over Gmail's 25MB limit? (Base64 encoding adds ~33%)
4. How do I check my PDF's file size?
5. What's the fastest fix — how do I compress a PDF for email?
6. What if the PDF is still too large after compressing? (split or cloud link)
7. Are there other reasons besides file size? (corrupted, antivirus, client bug, connection)
8. Can I send a large PDF without compressing? (cloud link alternative)

## Competitor angles + our gap

Competitors (gotoolsonline, pdfguru, freepdfconvert) focus almost entirely on "file too large" and miss the other causes. They also typically pitch upload-based tools. Our gap:
- Cover ALL causes, not just size
- Pitch the free in-browser compress tool (no upload, no sign-up)
- Give the specific email size limits in a clear table
- Explain the Base64 encoding overhead (why your 19MB file hits Gmail's 25MB cap)
