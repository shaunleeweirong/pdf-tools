## Key concepts / facts

- A booklet requires pages in multiples of 4 (each sheet = 4 page faces). A 10-page PDF needs 2 blank pages added.
- True booklet printing reorders pages: last page and page 1 share one side, pages 2 and 3 share the other side - then you fold and staple.
- 2-pages-per-sheet (N-up) keeps pages in reading order - good for handouts/study notes, not for saddle-stitch booklets.
- Most modern printers support duplex (double-sided) printing; without it you must flip pages manually.
- Chrome, Edge, and Firefox all have built-in booklet/multi-page-per-sheet options in their print dialogs - no Adobe needed.
- Mac Preview has a native "Two-sided" and "Two pages per sheet" option under Print.
