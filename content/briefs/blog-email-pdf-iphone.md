# Research Brief: how to email a pdf from iphone free

**targetQuery:** how to email a pdf from iphone free
**Secondary keywords:** send pdf from iphone, iphone email pdf attachment, attach pdf to email iphone, email pdf iphone gmail

## Search intent

The reader has a PDF on their iPhone and wants to send it by email right now. Most people won't know about edge cases — they just need to know where the Share button is and what to do when the file is too large.

## Real questions to answer (from PAA / forums)

1. How do I attach a PDF to an email on iPhone?
2. Where is the share/attach option in the Files app?
3. How do I email a PDF from Gmail on iPhone?

5. My PDF is too large to email — what do I do?
6. How do I compress a PDF on iPhone before sending?
7. When does PDF compression not work (text PDFs get bigger)?
8. What is Mail Drop and should I use it?
9. How do I send multiple PDFs at once from iPhone?

## Competitor gaps

Most guides cover only the Files app and stop there. They don't address:
- The Base64 encoding overhead that makes a 19 MB file fail a 25 MB limit
- That text-only PDFs get bigger when compressed, not smaller
- The practical difference between Mail Drop and cloud links
- Combining PDFs before sending

## Recommended format

How-to blog post with numbered steps, a size-limit table, and caveats about compression. Length: 850-1000 words.

## Internal link targets

## The "get it done" angle

Quick path first (Files app share), then size-limit context, then compression caveats so the reader doesn't waste time compressing a text document.
