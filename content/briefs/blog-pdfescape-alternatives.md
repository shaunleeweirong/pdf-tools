## PDFescape's actual limitations

- Files ARE uploaded to their servers for processing
- Free tier: 10 MB file size cap, 100 pages per document max
- Limited editing features (basic text, annotations, forms only)
- Occasional reliability/uptime issues reported
- Interface is dated compared to modern browser tools
- Privacy: your files sit on PDFescape servers during processing
