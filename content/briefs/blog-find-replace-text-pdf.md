## Methods (ordered by ease)

1. Google Docs: Upload to Drive, Open with Google Docs, Ctrl+H, download as PDF. Free, no install. Layout can shift on complex files.
2. Free online tool (PDF4me, Aspose): Upload PDF, type find/replace, download. No install, but file goes to a server.
3. Microsoft Word: Open PDF in Word (requires subscription), Ctrl+H, Save As PDF.
4. Direct editing with our edit-pdf tool: For 1-2 occurrences, manual editing is faster than any conversion.
