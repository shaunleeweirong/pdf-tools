## Key findings from research

- Google Slides has no native PDF import (not in supported import formats)
- Method 1 (image-based): convert pages to JPG with pdf-to-jpg, insert one per slide. Perfect visual fidelity, text not editable
- Method 2 (PPTX): convert PDF to .pptx (third-party tool needed), upload to Google Drive, open with Slides. Editable text, but fonts and layout may shift, especially on design-heavy PDFs
- Common pain: portrait PDF pages (A4/Letter) go into 16:9 landscape slides, causing gray bars
- Google Drive auto-converts .pptx to Slides format on open (no extra step)
- Batch image insert possible in Google Slides (shift-select multiple images)
