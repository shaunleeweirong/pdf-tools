## Why people are leaving Foxit
- Free tier features (PDF printer, some editing tools) moved to the paid PDF Editor subscription
- Desktop installer requires admin rights, which is a problem on corporate or shared machines
- Dense ribbon-style interface is over-engineered for quick one-off tasks
- Push toward subscriptions and upsells in the free version
- Data-governance questions raised after a 2024 acquisition prompted some enterprise users to look elsewhere

## Competitor analysis
Most alternatives pages focus on other desktop apps (SumatraPDF, PDF-XChange Editor, Adobe Acrobat Reader). Very few mention browser-based tools that need no install and no account. Our angle: for the everyday tasks people use Foxit for (merge, compress image PDFs, sign, protect, rotate), browser-based tools do the job free and private.
