## Key facts from research

- ChatGPT free plan: up to 3 file uploads per day; once daily GPT-4o quota is used, document upload stops working until reset
- Hard cap per file: 512 MB (text docs also limited to ~2M tokens, roughly 1,500 pages)
- ChatGPT Plus: up to 80 files every 3 hours
- Per-message limit raised to 20 files per message (Feb 2026 OpenAI release)
- Alternative to upload: paste the text from the PDF directly into the prompt box (no account needed)

## Competitor angles and our gaps

Competitors (makeuseof, tomsguide, updf) all cover the direct upload method. Most miss:
- Clear warning about what happens when you try to compress a text PDF before uploading (it gets bigger, not smaller)
- Practical advice on when to compress vs. split vs. paste text
- Specific measured numbers backing the compression advice

Our angle: honest guidance on which fix actually works for each file type, backed by measured numbers.
