## Key finding (critical for honest advice)

Our compress tool rasterizes pages to JPEG. For image-heavy resumes (headshots, graphic design templates, scans), this works well — a six-page image deck went from 913.4 KB to 315.3 KB at Medium (tool-measurements.md). For text-only resumes, it backfires badly — a 20-page text document went from 23.5 KB to 1,400.8 KB (tool-measurements.md). Never recommend compression for text resumes.

## Competitor gaps we beat

- None explain what actually makes a resume large or warn about ATS rasterization risk
- Our honest advice (re-export from source first; only compress if image-heavy) is more useful
