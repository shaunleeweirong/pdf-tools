## The key insight (gap vs. competitors)

Most competitor articles list 4-5 upload-based tools and do not explain **why** some watermarks are removable and others aren't. The real question is: **what type of watermark is it?**

Three types:
1. **Text overlay** (e.g., "DRAFT", "CONFIDENTIAL" as a PDF text element or annotation) — selectable, deletable with Edit PDF
2. **Image watermark** (logo/stamp embedded as an XObject image) — needs AI inpainting
3. **Burned-in** (part of scanned page pixels) — hardest; AI tools give mixed results

Our angle: answer the "why can't I click on it?" question first, give a free in-browser path for text watermarks (Edit PDF), and be honest about when you need external tools.

## Competitor gaps

- Don't explain the three watermark types
- Recommend uploading sensitive docs to cloud tools without noting privacy implications
- Don't mention that our Edit PDF tool works for removable text elements without upload
