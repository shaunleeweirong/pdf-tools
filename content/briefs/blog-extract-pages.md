## Competitor analysis

**iLovePDF / Smallpdf / PDF24:** All cover the how-to steps well. Most require uploading to their servers (cloud processing). None lead with the privacy/no-upload angle strongly.

**Gap we win on:** Our extract-pages tool runs in the browser. Files never leave the device. That's worth leading on, but as a trust cue in context, not as the headline.

**Also missing from most competitors:** Clear explanation of extract vs. split (people conflate them), and honest guidance on when NOT to use a browser tool (e.g., very large files may be slow).
