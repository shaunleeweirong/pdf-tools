## Search intent

User has a PDF with tabular data and wants to get it into a spreadsheet or structured format without retyping. They're looking for free options, no sign-up. Pain point is that PDFs don't behave like spreadsheets and copy-paste often breaks structure.

## Key distinction to address first

Two types of PDF:
1. Text-based (digitally created): text is embedded, can be selected in viewer, tools can read it
2. Scanned (image-based): pages are raster images, no text layer, standard extraction fails

## Real questions people ask (PAA / community mining)

1. How do I know if my PDF is text-based or scanned?
2. What's the fastest free way to extract a table?
3. Can Tabula extract from scanned PDFs? (No - common misconception)
4. How do I extract tables from scanned PDFs for free?
5. Are online table extractors safe for confidential documents?
6. Does extracting tables change the original PDF?

## Methods to cover

1. **Copy-paste from browser** - no tools needed, works for simple text-based tables
2. **Tabula** (tabula.technology) - best free desktop tool, open-source, local, text PDFs only, exports CSV/XLS
3. **Google Drive + Google Docs** - upload, open with Docs, tables often carry over
4. **Convert to image first** (for scanned PDFs) - extract the page, convert to JPG, paste into Google Sheets

## Competitor gap

Most posts either ignore the text vs. scanned distinction, or just list online tools without warning about privacy. We beat them by:
- Explaining the distinction upfront
- Being clear about Tabula's limitations
- Recommending a privacy-safe local workflow
- Linking to our tools where they genuinely help
