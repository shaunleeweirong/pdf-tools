## Measured facts to use

- Merging does not inflate file size: output stays close to sum of inputs (measured 971.9 KB in, 970.5 KB out)
- Compression makes text files larger, not smaller (23.5 KB text report -> 1.4 MB after compression) — mention as a caution
- Gmail and Outlook cap attachments at 25 MB; Base64 encoding adds ~33% so practical ceiling is ~18 MB
