## Key fact: do NOT compress before converting

The compressor rasterizes pages to JPEG (measured: 23.5 KB text PDF → 1.4 MB). Recommending compression before conversion would make extraction harder and the file bigger. This is NOT typical knowledge so it's a genuine differentiator to mention it.
