## Competitor/research findings

- **PDF Candy's main pain point:** one task per hour on the free tier. Paid plan removes it ($6/month or $48/year or $99 lifetime).
- PDF Candy uploads files to EU/USA servers, auto-deletes within 2 hours, AES-256 in transit/at rest.
- PDF Candy does NOT require a sign-up for basic tasks — that's a genuine positive we should acknowledge.
- PDF Candy has 90+ tools including OCR, format conversions (PDF to Word/Excel/PPT) — we don't cover those.
- Competing alternatives pages (g2, alternativeto, saasworthy) mention foxit, adobe, nitro (paid), and browser-local tools.

## Our angle / gap to beat

PDF Candy's main friction is the one-task-per-hour throttle and server upload. Browser-based tools have neither: no server to throttle means no hourly cap, and in-browser processing means files never travel to a third party. The key differentiation is architectural (not policy).
