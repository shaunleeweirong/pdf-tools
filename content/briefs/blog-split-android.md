## Key findings from research

- Browser-based tools are the dominant recommendation: smallpdf, ilovepdf, pdf24, pdfcdf, and our own split-pdf tool all work in Chrome on Android.
- Chrome has a built-in trick: open the PDF in Chrome, print to PDF, and enter a custom page range. This produces a new PDF with only those pages. It requires no extra software.
- Pain points from users: many "free" apps are actually freemium with watermarks; some require sign-in; store apps take up space for a one-time task.
- The in-browser angle (no install, no upload, no sign-in) is the strongest differentiator.
- A newer concern: file privacy. Users ask whether the tool uploads files. Client-side processing is a meaningful differentiator.
