## Our workflow

1. Extract Pages tool → saves the target page as a one-page PDF
2. Merge PDF tool → merges original + extracted page at desired position
3. For mid-document insertion: split at insertion point, then merge (first half, copy, second half)
4. Mac alternative: Preview built-in (Cmd+C / Cmd+V in thumbnail view)
