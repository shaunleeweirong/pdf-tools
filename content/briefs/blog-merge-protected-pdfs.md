## Search intent
Someone has two or more PDF files. Some or all are password-protected. They're trying to drag them into a PDF merge tool and it either asks for a password or simply blocks the operation. They want to know: can I do this, and how?

## Real questions people ask (PAA / Reddit / Adobe forums)
1. Why can't I combine password protected PDF files?
2. Do I need to remove the password before merging?
3. What's the difference between an open password and a permissions password?
4. Can I merge PDFs that I can't edit?
5. What happens to form fields when I merge PDFs?
6. Can I merge PDFs without knowing the password?
7. Will merging change the file size?
8. Can I merge multiple password-protected PDFs at the same time?

## Key facts from tool-measurements.md to cite
- Merging three files totalling 971.9 KB produced 970.5 KB output (essentially no size change)
- Merging strips interactive form fields; page content survives but fields disappear
- Recommendation: flatten filled forms before merging

## Competitor gaps
- Most pages list 4-5 tools (Adobe, WPS, iLovePDF) without explaining WHY protection blocks merging
- None clearly explain the open password vs. permissions password distinction
- None warn about form fields being stripped
- Our privacy advantage: files never leave the browser (in-browser processing)

## Recommended format
Blog how-to post. Two main action steps (remove password, merge). Plus explanations of: why it happens, two password types, form field warning, what to do if you don't have the password.
