## Competitor gaps

Top-ranking pages (Adobe, iLovePDF, Smallpdf) all push their own paid/registration-required tools. They mention merging but don't explain the image-to-PDF then merge two-step for photo pages. They don't explain the form-field caveat. Gap we win: free, no account, and honest about what merging does to form fields (flatten first).

## Measured facts to use

- Merge is size-neutral: 971.9 KB in → 970.5 KB out (three files)
- Merge strips interactive form fields (AcroForm not preserved)
- Flatten bakes field values into page content before merging
