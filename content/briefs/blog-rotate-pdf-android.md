## Key distinction to explain (competitor gap)

Most articles skip the most important thing: view rotation vs permanent rotation. Many basic PDF viewers (including the Chrome PDF viewer on Android) let you spin the display temporarily, but don't write the change to the file. Close and reopen, and it's sideways again. Competitors mention this briefly but don't explain why it happens.

The technical explanation: a permanent rotation writes a value to the `/Rotate` entry in each page's dictionary as defined by ISO 32000. View-only rotation changes nothing in the file.
