## Competitor angles + our gap

- Most posts explain the mechanics (right-click on Windows, Compress on Mac) but don't explain WHY zipping usually doesn't shrink PDFs much.
- None clearly answers the "zip vs merge vs compress" question, which is the decision most readers are actually trying to make.
- Our angle: teach readers when zipping is the right move (bundling many files, keeping them separate) and when our in-browser tools (Merge PDF, Compress PDF) are better for size reduction.

## Important constraints from tool-measurements.md

- Never recommend Compress PDF for text-based PDFs (23.5 KB text PDF grew to 1.4 MB). Only recommend for image-heavy/scanned PDFs.
- Merge PDF does not inflate file size (971.9 KB in, 970.5 KB out), strips form fields.
- Do not claim zip shrinks PDFs significantly - PDFs are already internally compressed.
