## Key findings from research

- SumatraPDF: ~6 MB installer, open-source, opens in under a second, no sign-up ever, portable mode available. No annotations, no forms, no JavaScript.
- Microsoft Edge: built into Windows 10 and 11, zero downloads, basic reading + highlighting + inking + Read Aloud. Handles basic form fields.
- Foxit PDF Reader: ~190 MB install, free with no account required. Full annotations, form filling, signatures. Has upsell prompts for paid Editor, can ignore.
- PDF-XChange Editor: free tier, most feature-rich. Annotations, OCR, markup. Stamps watermark on pages you export after editing (not on read-only pages).
- Adobe Acrobat Reader: ~240 MB, no account required to read. Most compatible with complex PDFs (JavaScript, digital signatures). Upsells heavily.
- Chrome / Firefox: built-in PDF viewer, good for quick reads, no annotations.

## Competitor gap
