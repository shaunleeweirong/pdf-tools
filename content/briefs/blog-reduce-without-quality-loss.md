## Competitor angles + our gap

Competitors (smallpdf, Adobe, PDF24, Canva) all lead with "upload and compress." They bury the nuance about text vs. image PDFs and don't explain lossless vs lossy. Our gap:
- Explain the mechanics clearly (lossless = redundant data; lossy = image re-encode)
- Lead with the fact that for most PDFs, you lose no visible quality
- Emphasize browser-based (files never uploaded) — privacy + convenience
- Honest about when compression DOES affect quality (high-compression on scanned docs)
