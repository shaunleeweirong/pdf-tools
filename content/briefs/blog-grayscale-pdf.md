## Why people want this

- Printing cost savings: color ink costs 5 to 10x more than monochrome. A page at 12-15¢ (color) vs. 3-5¢ (black and white).
- Document appearance: grayscale looks professional and clean for contracts, reports, worksheets.
- File size: some methods reduce size (image-heavy PDFs), others increase it (re-rendering text pages).

## Competitor gaps

- Most pages list tools without explaining when grayscale helps vs. hurts file size.

- Privacy: few mention client-side options (files never leave browser).
