## Methods to cover

1. **Microsoft Word** (File > Open > PDF) — free if you have M365 or Office. Good for text PDFs, bad for scans and complex layouts.
2. **Google Docs** (upload to Drive, open with Docs, download as .docx) — totally free, no paid tier needed.
3. **Free online converter** (iLovePDF, Smallpdf) — no install, handles OCR for scanned PDFs, some daily limits.
