## Key findings from research

- macOS Preview is built-in and free. Steps: Markup toolbar > Rectangular Selection > select area > Tools > Crop (Cmd+K) > Save.
- CRITICAL GAP: Preview is non-destructive. Cropped content is hidden, not deleted. A PDF editor can reveal it. Most competitor posts bury or skip this. This is our angle.
- Preview crops one page at a time. No "apply to all pages" option. This is a real pain point for multi-page docs.
- Our /crop-pdf tool trims margins from every page at once, runs in the browser, no sign-up.
- For permanent removal of sensitive content: cropping is not redaction. Different tools needed.
