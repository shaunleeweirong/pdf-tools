## Search intent

Reader has a PDF link or file and wants to read it without saving it to their computer. Three sub-intents:
1. Their browser is set to download instead of open PDFs - they want to fix the setting.
2. They want to open a PDF from someone else's link without downloading it.
3. They want to share a PDF so other people can view it in-browser without downloading.

## Real questions to answer

From PAA / Google Chrome Community / Dropbox Community:

1. Do modern browsers open PDFs automatically without downloading?
2. How do I stop Chrome / Firefox / Edge from downloading PDFs instead of opening them?
3. How do I view a PDF that a website is forcing me to download?
4. Can I view a PDF from Google Drive without downloading it?
5. What free online PDF viewer works without sign-up?
6. How do I share a PDF so others can view it online without downloading?

## Competitor gaps

Competitor posts (swifdoo, flippingbook) cover the browser-settings fix but:
- Bury the browser settings step under ads/tool pitches
- Don't distinguish "I can't open a PDF" vs "a website is forcing download"
- Don't mention that Google Drive serves as a universal online viewer (paste any URL via the Drive viewer trick)
- Don't explain when to compress/split first so the PDF loads faster in browser

Our angle: answer-first, cover all three sub-intents clearly, no forced tool pitch.
