## Key facts to cover

- Double-sided (duplex) printing is a hardware feature. If the printer lacks a duplex unit, automatic two-sided is not possible.
- On Windows: open PDF in Chrome, Edge, or the free Adobe Acrobat Reader > Ctrl+P > open printer preferences/properties > find Duplex or Two-sided setting.
- On Mac: Preview > File > Print > expand the panel > check "Two-Sided" (or find it under Layout drop-down).
- Flip on long edge = book-style (pages flip along the left/right edge). Best for portrait-orientation documents.
- Flip on short edge = calendar-style (pages flip along the top edge). Best for landscape-orientation documents.
- Manual duplex (printers without auto-duplex): print odd pages, flip the stack, print even pages. Chrome's print dialog has a "Print on both sides" option that will guide you through this on some printers.
- The "double-sided" option may be missing if: the printer driver is the generic Windows driver instead of the full manufacturer driver, or the printer model simply does not have a duplex unit.
