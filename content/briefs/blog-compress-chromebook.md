## Competitor angles and gaps

- Most competitor pages (Smallpdf, Adobe, DocHub, FreeCompress) only list generic methods and push sign-ups or paid plans.
- None clearly warn that the compressor makes text PDFs dramatically larger (a critical error users make repeatedly).
- None distinguish well between image-heavy PDFs (where compression helps) and text PDFs (where it backfires badly).
- Adobe's guide mentions Android apps as an option, but Chromebook users can avoid that entirely.
- Our gap to exploit: honesty about when NOT to compress, the in-browser/no-upload angle, and the privacy benefit.
