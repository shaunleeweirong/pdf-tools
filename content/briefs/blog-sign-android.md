## Competitor angles and gaps

- Adobe, SignEasy, Xodo guides require app installation or an account.
- Most guides don't explain the difference between electronic and digital signatures.
- Privacy angle is underplayed: few guides mention that most apps upload your document.
- Our tool is browser-based (no upload, no account) — this is the clear gap to win on.
