Printing a PDF from Google Drive is quick once you know where to look. Open the file in the Drive viewer and you can send it to any connected printer without downloading anything first.

Here's everything you need, split by device.

## How to print a PDF from Google Drive on a computer

Google Drive has a built-in PDF viewer in every major browser. You don't need Adobe Reader, a Chrome extension, or any other app.

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com) and sign in.
2. Find the PDF and double-click it. It opens in the Drive viewer inside your browser tab.
3. Press **Ctrl+P** (Windows or Chromebook) or **Cmd+P** (Mac) to open the print dialog.
4. Choose your printer, set the number of copies, pick a page range if you only need part of the document, and click **Print**.

If keyboard shortcuts aren't your thing, there's also a printer icon in the top-right toolbar of the viewer. Clicking it does the same thing.

**Downloading is optional.** The print dialog talks directly to your printer, so the PDF stays in Drive. Nothing is saved locally unless you want it to be.

## How to print from Google Drive on iPhone or iPad

You'll need the Google Drive app (free on the App Store). Once it's installed:

1. Open the Drive app and find your PDF.
2. Tap the three-dot menu (the icon that looks like three dots stacked vertically) next to the file name.
3. Tap **Share and export**.
4. Tap **Print**.
5. In the AirPrint picker, select your printer. Make sure your iPhone and the printer are on the same Wi-Fi network.
6. Tap the print button in the top-right corner.

[AirPrint](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111916) is Apple's built-in wireless printing system, built into iOS since 2010. It works with most printers sold in the last several years. If yours isn't showing up, check the printer manufacturer's site for an iOS print app, which gives you a direct connection instead.

## How to print from Google Drive on Android

1. Open the **Google Drive** app on your Android phone or tablet.
2. Tap the three-dot menu next to the PDF.
3. Tap **Share and export**, then tap **Print**.
4. Android's printing system scans your network and lists compatible printers. Tap yours.
5. Adjust settings like copies or page range, then tap the print icon to confirm.

Android's default [print system](https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/7225252) supports many common printer brands out of the box. If your printer isn't appearing, search the Google Play Store for your printer brand's official plugin (HP Print Service, Canon Print Service, etc.) and install it. After that, your printer shows up in the Android print picker.

## What to do when the PDF is too large to open in Drive

Google Drive's in-browser viewer handles PDFs up to 100 MB. Files bigger than that won't load for preview, and you won't be able to print them from the viewer.

The fix is to shrink the file first, then re-upload it.

Our [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf) tool runs entirely in your browser, which means your file never gets sent to any server. It works best on PDFs that contain scanned pages or images. If your PDF is mostly typed text, compression won't help and may actually make the file larger, because the tool converts pages to JPEG images. In that case, try removing some pages or splitting the document into smaller chunks with our [Split PDF](/split-pdf) tool instead.

Once the file is under Google's size limit, upload the compressed version to Drive and print from there.

## Printing multiple PDFs from Google Drive at once

Drive doesn't have a built-in batch-print feature. You can't select ten files and send them all to the printer in one go.

The practical workaround: combine your PDFs into a single file first, then print that one file. Our [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf) tool does this in your browser. Add your files in the order you want them to print, then download the merged PDF, upload it to Drive (or just print it directly from your browser), and you're done.

Merging doesn't add file bloat. The merged output comes in very close to the sum of the individual files.

## Troubleshooting: why won't my PDF print from Google Drive?

A few things that commonly get in the way:

**No printer appears in the dialog.** On a desktop, check that your OS has a default printer set. On Windows, go to Settings, then Bluetooth and devices, then Printers and scanners. On Mac, check System Settings under Printers and Scanners.

**The print dialog is blocked.** Some browsers treat the print dialog as a popup. If you see a blocked-popup icon in the address bar, click it and allow popups for drive.google.com.

**The PDF has print restrictions.** Some PDFs are locked by their creator to prevent printing. You'll need to remove those restrictions before you can print.

**The file is corrupted.** If the viewer shows a blank page or an error, the PDF may have been damaged during upload. Try re-downloading the original file and re-uploading it to Drive.

**On mobile, you're using a browser instead of the app.** The Drive mobile website in Safari or Chrome is stripped down compared to the app. If printing isn't working from your phone's browser, install the Google Drive app and try again from there.

If you've ruled everything out and it still won't print, download the PDF to your device and open it in your system's built-in PDF viewer: Preview on Mac, or Microsoft Edge on Windows. Both have a File menu with a Print option and tend to be more reliable than browser-based printing for tricky files.
