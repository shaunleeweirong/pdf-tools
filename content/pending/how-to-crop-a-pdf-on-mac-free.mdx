## Two free ways to crop a PDF on Mac

You can crop a PDF on Mac for free without paying for Acrobat. The two practical options are macOS's built-in [Preview](https://support.apple.com/guide/preview/crop-or-rotate-a-pdf-prvw11567/mac) app and a browser-based PDF tool that handles every page at once. Which one fits depends on a detail most guides skip, so let's get that out of the way first.

## How to crop a PDF on Mac with Preview

Preview is installed on every Mac. No download, no account, and it opens PDF files by default.

1. Open your PDF in Preview. If it opens in another app, right-click the file and choose Open With > Preview.
2. Click the **Markup toolbar** button (the pencil icon near the top right of the window). If you don't see it, go to View > Show Markup Toolbar.
3. Click the **Rectangular Selection** tool (the dotted rectangle icon on the left side of the toolbar).
4. Click and drag across the page to select the area you want to keep.
5. Go to **Tools > Crop** in the menu bar, or press **Cmd+K**.
6. Press **Cmd+S** to save.

The page now shows only the area you selected. For a single-page PDF or a quick one-off job, this takes about 30 seconds.

The problem: if you have a 15-page document and want the same crop applied to every page, you're doing that 15 times. Preview has no "apply to all pages" option.

## The catch: Preview's crop doesn't permanently remove content

This is the part most tutorials leave out, and it matters.

When you crop a PDF in Preview, the content outside your selected area is *hidden*, not deleted. The full original content stays in the file. Open the same PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader, PDF Expert, or almost any other PDF tool, and you can drag the crop box back out to reveal what was there.

For trimming white margins off a scanned document before printing, that's fine. The hidden content is just blank space, and there's nothing sensitive to protect.

But if you're trying to hide something, cropping in Preview won't do it. A company logo you cropped out, a handwritten note in the margin, an internal URL in the footer, all of it is still accessible to anyone with a PDF editor. For that kind of job, you need [document redaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanitization_(classified_information)), not a crop tool.

## How to crop all pages of a PDF on Mac at once

If your goal is to trim consistent margins off every page of a multi-page document (a scanned textbook, a form with oversized borders, a report with wide margins), Preview isn't the right tool.

Our [Crop PDF](/crop-pdf) tool handles the whole document in one pass. It runs in your browser, there's no sign-up, and your file doesn't leave your device.

1. Open [Crop PDF](/crop-pdf) in Safari, Chrome, or any browser on your Mac.
2. Drop your PDF onto the upload area or click to select a file.
3. Adjust the crop handles to set the margins you want removed. The preview updates as you drag.
4. Click to apply and download the result.

The tool trims the same margins from every page, so you get consistent output across the whole document without repeating the process page by page.

## Preview vs. browser tool: which to use

| Situation | Best option |
|---|---|
| Cropping a single page | Preview |
| Quick crop, no internet | Preview |
| Trimming margins from all pages at once | [Crop PDF](/crop-pdf) |
| Sensitive content you need permanently removed | Dedicated redaction tool |
| You need a specific crop area, not just margins | Preview |

Preview is faster for one-page jobs or when you're offline. The browser tool saves you time when you have more than a couple of pages or need a consistent crop across the document.

## Cropping is not the same as compressing

A common mix-up: people crop a PDF hoping it will shrink the file size. Cropping changes what's visible on the page, not the number of bytes the file takes up. A 4 MB PDF stays roughly 4 MB after cropping because the underlying content is still there.

If you need to reduce file size for emailing or uploading, use our [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf) tool instead. If your PDF contains photos or scanned images, compression can reduce the file significantly. Keep in mind that the compressor works by rasterizing pages to JPEG, so it works best on image-heavy files, not on text documents.

## If your PDF has form fields

Cropping a PDF that has interactive form fields won't delete those fields. They'll still exist in the file, just possibly outside the visible area. If you've filled out a form and want to lock the values before sharing, flatten the PDF first using our [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf) tool, then crop or save as needed.

## Quick summary

Cropping a PDF on Mac is free either way. Use Preview for a fast, single-page crop when you're offline or just need to snip some white space. Use a browser-based tool when you want to apply the same trim to every page in one step. And if the content you're removing is sensitive, remember that a crop tool hides it but doesn't delete it from the file.
