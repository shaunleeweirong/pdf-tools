## The fastest method: convert JPG to PDF in your browser (no install)

You can convert a JPG to PDF on Android for free right now, with nothing to install. Open Chrome (or any browser) on your phone, go to the [Images to PDF tool](/jpg-to-pdf), and tap "Select files". Choose your photos from the gallery, and the tool builds the PDF in your browser. Tap download and it lands in your Downloads folder.

A few things that make this different from most approaches:

- **Multiple images at once.** Select several JPGs in the gallery picker and get a single PDF back, one image per page, in the order you chose.
- **No upload.** The conversion runs entirely in your browser. Your photos are not sent to any server, which matters when the images are personal, medical, or work-related.
- **Free, no account.** There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.

The tool accepts JPG and PNG files. If your photos are screenshots or PNG images from a messaging app, they work the same way.

## Why convert a photo to PDF at all?

PDFs are what email attachments, submission portals, and government forms expect. A [PDF (Portable Document Format)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDF) keeps the content looking the same on every device, whether it opens on a phone, a laptop, or a printer. JPGs can display differently depending on the viewer and lose quality if re-saved. For sending a photo of your ID, a signed document, or a set of receipts, a PDF is the more dependable format.

## How to use Android's built-in print feature

Android has a "Save as PDF" option built into its print system. The [Android print framework](https://developer.android.com/training/printing) lets any app that supports printing also save to PDF. The Gallery and Google Photos both support this.

Here's how to do it:

1. Open Gallery (or Google Photos) and tap the photo you want.
2. Tap the three-dot menu (or the share icon, depending on your phone).
3. Tap **Print**.
4. In the printer dropdown at the top, choose **Save as PDF**.
5. Tap the Save button (the PDF icon in the top right) and choose a folder.

The file goes to your Downloads folder by default. Samsung Galaxy phones label some menu items differently from Pixel phones, but the Print option appears in the same share sheet on all of them.

One limitation: this method converts one image per run. If you need three photos in one PDF, you'd repeat the process three times and then combine the results with the [Merge PDF tool](/merge-pdf), or just use the browser tool from the start.

## How to use Google Drive to convert a photo to PDF

If you already use [Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/6154780), there's a path through the app that many people miss:

1. Open Google Drive on your Android.
2. Tap the "+" (Add) button.
3. Choose **Scan** to photograph a document, or **Upload** to pick an existing image from your gallery.
4. After the image appears in Drive, tap the three-dot menu and select **Print**.
5. Change the printer to **Save as PDF** and tap Save.

The Scan option works best for paper documents because it applies corrections to straighten edges and improve contrast. For photos you've already taken, uploading and then printing gives more faithful results.

This route makes sense when you're already sending the file to someone via Drive, since the PDF goes straight to your Google Drive folder and you can share it from there.

## How to combine multiple photos into one PDF on Android

The built-in print method converts one image at a time, which is fine for a single photo. For a batch, two options work better:

**Browser tool (easiest):** Open the [Images to PDF tool](/jpg-to-pdf) in your browser, tap "Select files", and long-press in the gallery picker to select several photos at once. The tool puts them into one PDF in the order you chose. Download it and you're done.

**Merge route:** If your photos are already separate PDFs (from a previous conversion, say), the [Merge PDF tool](/merge-pdf) lets you combine them into one file. Drag them into order, download the merged PDF. This is useful when you've already converted some pages and need to add more later.
