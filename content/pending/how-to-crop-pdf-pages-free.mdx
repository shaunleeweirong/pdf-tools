## The quickest way to crop PDF pages for free

Cropping a PDF trims its visible margins, and you can do it free in any browser without creating an account. Whether you want to cut thick white borders from a document, clean up the dark edge of a scan, or tighten up slides before sharing, a browser-based tool handles it in a few clicks.

The [Crop PDF tool](/crop-pdf) runs directly in your browser. Your file is never sent to a server.

## What cropping a PDF actually does

When you crop a PDF, you're adjusting the page's visible boundary. In [PDF terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDF), this boundary is called the CropBox, and it tells your PDF viewer which portion of the page to display. Drag the crop handles inward and the viewer shows a smaller, tighter rectangle of the page.

One thing worth knowing: cropping affects what you see, not always what is stored in the file. Content outside the crop boundary may remain in the document data depending on the tool. If you're trying to permanently remove sensitive information from a page, a dedicated redaction tool is the right approach, not a crop.

## How to crop PDF pages free

Use the [Crop PDF tool](/crop-pdf) to trim margins from every page at once:

1. Go to the [Crop PDF tool](/crop-pdf) and click "Choose file," or drag your PDF onto the page.
2. A preview appears. Drag the handles on each side to set the crop boundary.
3. Click "Crop" to apply that boundary to all pages.
4. Download the trimmed PDF.

No account. No watermark. The tool applies the same crop to every page, so a uniform margin trim takes one step.

## How to remove white margins from a PDF

Large white margins make documents harder to read on tablets and e-readers, and they waste paper when printed. To trim them:

- Drag the crop handles inward until they sit just outside the text or content area.
- Leave a small buffer of a few millimeters so nothing gets accidentally clipped.
- Before downloading, check a page that has images or running headers, since those often sit closer to the edge than body text.

If you're working with a multi-column paper or a textbook, keep an eye on footnotes at the bottom and headers at the top when you set the boundary.

## How to remove black borders from a scanned PDF

Scanned pages often carry a dark shadow around the edges from the scanner glass. It doesn't affect the text but looks messy in shared files.

To clean it up:

1. Set the crop boundary just inside the dark border on each side.
2. If the shadow is thicker on one side (common with flatbed scanners), adjust each edge individually.
3. Preview the result before downloading to confirm no actual content is being clipped.

This works well for receipts, signed contracts, and document archives that were scanned in bulk.

## Can I crop only one page, or will all pages change?

The [Crop PDF tool](/crop-pdf) applies the trim to every page. That's what most people need for uniform margin removal across a report, a book, or a batch of scanned forms.

If you only need to crop a specific page, the workaround is to split the PDF first, crop the individual page, then merge everything back together. The [Split PDF](/split-pdf) and [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf) tools are both free and run in your browser.

## Does cropping a PDF make it smaller?

Not by much, if at all. Cropping adjusts the visible boundary on each page but doesn't strip the image or text data that's already embedded in the file. Opening the cropped PDF and checking its size, you'll find it close to the original.

To actually shrink a PDF, the [Compress PDF tool](/compress-pdf) is the right tool. That said, it works best on files with photos or image-heavy pages. For a text-only document like a contract or report, the compression step rasterizes every page to an image, which makes the file larger rather than smaller. If reducing a text PDF's size matters to you, splitting it into smaller sections or using a ZIP archive is a better path.

## How to crop a PDF on Mac for free

Mac users have two options that cost nothing:

**Preview (already on your Mac):** Open the PDF in [Preview](https://support.apple.com/guide/preview/welcome/mac), draw a selection rectangle over the area you want to keep, then go to Tools > Crop. This trims one page at a time. For a quick single-page adjustment, it's the fastest route with nothing to download.

**Browser-based:** For a multi-page document, the [Crop PDF tool](/crop-pdf) in Safari or Chrome is faster because it applies the crop to all pages in one pass.

## How to crop a PDF without Adobe Acrobat

Acrobat can crop PDFs, but it's a paid subscription. Free alternatives handle most common tasks just as well:

- **In the browser:** [Crop PDF tool](/crop-pdf) (free, no sign-up, files stay private)
- **On Mac:** Preview (built in, no download needed)
- **On Windows:** [PDF24](https://tools.pdf24.org/en/crop-pdf) runs in the browser with nothing to install

The browser-based approach works on any operating system and any device. You don't install anything, you don't create an account, and your file doesn't leave your machine.
