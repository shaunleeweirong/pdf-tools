## Do online PDF tools upload your file?

Most do. The typical workflow for a server-side tool is: your file travels to their server, gets processed there, and the result is sent back to you. For a blank template that's fine. For a bank statement, a contract, or a medical document, it's worth thinking about.

The [Delete Pages tool](/delete-pages) on this site runs entirely in your browser. Your file goes from your device into your browser's memory, the pages are removed using the [browser's built-in file handling](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/File_API), and the result goes straight to your downloads. No server ever touches the content.

If you're using any other tool and privacy matters, look for explicit "in-browser" or "local processing" language on their page. Tools that skip this detail are likely uploading your file.

## How to delete PDF pages on a Mac for free

Mac users have a built-in option that most people overlook: the **Preview** app.

1. Open the PDF in Preview (it's the default PDF viewer on macOS).
2. If the sidebar isn't visible, go to **View > Thumbnails**.
3. Click the thumbnail of the page you want to remove.
4. Hold **Command** to select multiple pages at once.
5. Press **Delete** (or right-click and choose **Delete**).
6. Save with **Command + S**.

Preview overwrites the original file on save, so if you want to keep a copy with all pages intact, duplicate the file before you start.

For Windows, mobile, or anything more complex than a basic deletion, the [Delete Pages tool](/delete-pages) works the same in any modern browser on any device.

## Can you delete a range of pages all at once?

Yes. Most browser-based tools let you select non-consecutive pages by clicking thumbnails, or enter a page range directly (like "3-7") to remove an entire run of pages in one step.

The [Delete Pages tool](/delete-pages) uses the thumbnail approach, which is easiest when you can see what you're cutting. If your document is long and you already know the page numbers, look for a tool that accepts a typed range.

## What about blank pages from a scan?

Scanners often add blank pages at the end, especially when scanning double-sided originals. The fix is the same as removing any other page.

The thumbnail grid makes blank pages obvious: they appear white and empty. Select them all, delete, and download. If you're combining multiple scans first, the [Merge PDF tool](/merge-pdf) can bring them together before you clean up the blanks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I delete PDF pages without Acrobat?

Yes. Adobe Acrobat is not required. The [Delete Pages tool](/delete-pages) is free, runs in your browser, and needs no software download.

### Will deleting pages make the PDF file smaller?

Usually. Each page in a [PDF document](https://pdfa.org/resource/pdf-specification/) carries its content, embedded fonts, and rendering data. Removing pages typically shrinks the file. If you want to reduce it further after trimming, run the result through the [Compress PDF tool](/compress-pdf).

### Is it safe to delete pages from a confidential PDF online?

It depends on the tool. Server-side tools can see your document during processing. Tools that run entirely in your browser, including [Delete Pages](/delete-pages) on this site, never send your file anywhere, so they're a safer choice for anything sensitive.

### Can I undo a deletion after downloading?

No. The download is the final result. If you might want those pages back, keep a copy of the original before you start.

### What if I only want to keep a few pages out of a long document?

If you're keeping fewer pages than you're removing, it's faster to extract what you want than to delete everything else. Use the [Split PDF tool](/split-pdf) or the [Extract Pages tool](/extract-pages) to pull out only the pages you need.

### Can I also reorder pages while I'm editing the document?

Yes. The [Organize PDF tool](/organize-pdf) lets you drag pages into a new order, remove any you don't want, and save the result in one go. It's the better choice when you're doing both tasks at once.
