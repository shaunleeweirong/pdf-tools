You can sign a PDF on Android for free without installing anything. Open Chrome, go to the [Sign PDF tool](/sign-pdf), draw your signature directly on the touchscreen, and download the signed file in seconds. No Adobe account, no Play Store app, no upload to a third-party server.

## Why most PDF signing apps aren't worth installing

Search the Play Store for "PDF signer" and you'll find dozens of results. Most of them share the same problems: they upload your document to a remote server, require creating an account, and put core features behind a subscription.

If you're signing a contract, a rental form, or any document you'd rather keep private, sending it to a server you don't control is unnecessary. Your file should stay on your phone.

A browser-based tool handles things differently. The processing happens inside Chrome's JavaScript engine on your phone. Your file never leaves your device, there's no account to create, and there's nothing to install.

## How to sign a PDF on Android using Chrome

You need Chrome or any modern Android browser. The tool works on Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet, and Edge for Android.

1. Open Chrome on your Android device.
2. Go to the [Sign PDF tool](/sign-pdf).
3. Tap the upload area and select your PDF from your files, Google Drive, or Downloads.
4. Draw your signature in the signature box using your finger or a stylus.
5. Tap to apply the signature and generate the signed PDF.
6. Tap Download to save the file to your phone.

The signed file lands in your Downloads folder and is ready to share.

## Tips for drawing a clean signature on a touchscreen

Drawing with your finger takes a few tries to get right. A few things that actually help:

- **Turn your phone sideways.** The signature box is wider when your screen is horizontal, giving you more room to write naturally.
- **Slow down.** Fast swipes produce sharp, angular corners. Slower movements give you smoother curves.
- **Use a stylus if you have one.** Any capacitive stylus works on most Android touchscreens. Samsung Galaxy devices with an S Pen give you finer control than a fingertip.
- **Clear and retry.** The tool lets you clear the canvas and redraw as many times as you need before saving.
- **Zoom in if the canvas feels small.** Pinch to zoom in on the browser page to make the signature box larger, then draw.

## Do I need an Adobe account to sign a PDF on Android?

No. Adobe Acrobat Reader on Android requires you to sign in with an Adobe ID, and some signing features need a paid plan. If you just need to draw a signature and place it on a PDF, the [Sign PDF tool](/sign-pdf) has no login requirement and no paywall.

No email address, no password, no credit card.

## What "electronic signature" actually means

An [electronic signature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_signature) is any digital mark that shows your intent to agree to a document. Drawing your name on a touchscreen qualifies as one. In the United States, the [ESIGN Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Signatures_in_Global_and_National_Commerce_Act) gives electronic signatures the same legal weight as handwritten ones in most everyday situations.

A *digital signature* is a different thing. That term refers to a cryptographic certificate that ties an identity to a document in a verifiable, tamper-evident way. Most routine signing (lease agreements, consent forms, school permission slips, freelance contracts) doesn't require a cryptographic certificate. A drawn electronic signature is fine.

If someone specifically asks for a PKI-based digital certificate, a drawing tool won't satisfy that requirement. For day-to-day documents, it works.

## Is it safe to sign documents in a browser on Android?

With a client-side browser tool, your file never leaves your phone. The browser handles everything locally. That's a meaningful difference from:

- Apps that send your document to a remote server for processing
- Services that store your file against your account indefinitely
- Free apps that show few details about what happens to your data

A sensible precaution: before uploading a sensitive document, confirm you're on the right page. Navigate to the tool directly rather than tapping a search ad, or bookmark it after your first visit.

## How to save and share the signed PDF from Android

After you tap Download, the signed PDF goes to your Downloads folder. From there:

**Gmail:** Compose a message, tap the paperclip icon, tap Attach file, and pick the file from Downloads.

**WhatsApp:** Open a chat, tap the attachment icon, choose Document, and select the file from Downloads.

**Google Drive:** Open Drive, tap the plus button, choose Upload, and select the file. Then share the Drive link with whoever needs it.

**Other apps:** In your file manager, long-press the PDF and tap Share. Choose the app from the share sheet.

## What if the signature line is on a page other than the first?

The [Sign PDF tool](/sign-pdf) places your signature on the first page of the document. If the signature line is on a later page, the quickest fix is to use the [Organize PDF tool](/organize-pdf) to move that page to the front, sign it, and then reorder the pages back.

If the PDF has fillable form fields, try the [Fill PDF Form tool](/fill-form) instead. It lets you interact with fields on any page, including signature fields.

## After signing: flatten or leave as is?

Once you sign and download the file, the signature is part of the PDF. If you want to make sure no one can remove or alter it after the fact, the [Flatten PDF tool](/flatten-pdf) locks all annotations (including signatures) into the page so they can't be edited.

This matters most when you're sending a document for someone else to countersign. If you're only signing for your own records, flattening is optional.
