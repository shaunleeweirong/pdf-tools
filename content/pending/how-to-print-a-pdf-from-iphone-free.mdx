To print a PDF from your iPhone for free, the fastest method is AirPrint: open the file, tap the Share button, and tap Print. You need a compatible wireless printer. If yours does not support AirPrint, the major printer manufacturers all offer free iOS apps that do the same job.

Here is how each method works, along with fixes for the most common problems.

## How to print a PDF using AirPrint

[AirPrint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AirPrint) is Apple's built-in wireless printing system, available on every iPhone since iOS 4.2. It works over WiFi with no app installs or driver downloads.

1. Open the PDF in the Files app, Mail, Safari, or any app with a Share button.
2. Tap the **Share** icon (the box with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap **Print**.
4. Tap **Printer** and choose your printer from the list.
5. Set the number of copies and any other options.
6. Tap **Print** in the top-right corner.

The key requirement: your iPhone and your printer must be on the same WiFi network. Many home routers broadcast two bands (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz), and if your phone is on one and the printer is on the other, AirPrint will not find the printer. Put both on the same band and try again.

AirPrint is supported by hundreds of models from HP, Epson, Canon, Brother, and Lexmark. If your printer has a WiFi card and was made in the last several years, it is likely compatible. You can confirm by searching "[your printer model] AirPrint" on the manufacturer's website.

## What if my printer does not support AirPrint?

Older printers often lack AirPrint support, but the major brands offer free iOS apps that let you print wirelessly all the same.

- **HP Smart** (free, App Store): Works with most HP OfficeJet, DeskJet, LaserJet, and ENVY models. Open HP Smart, add your printer, then use the Share sheet from any app to send the PDF to HP Smart for printing.
- **Epson iPrint** (free): Covers Epson EcoTank, WorkForce, and Expression printers. The workflow is the same as HP Smart.
- **Canon PRINT** (free): Supports most Canon PIXMA and MAXIFY models released since around 2013.
- **Brother iPrint&Scan** (free): Handles Brother inkjet and laser printers, including older models without AirPrint.

To use any of these, download the app for your brand, add your printer while both are on the same network, and send the PDF to the app using the Share sheet.

## Printing from specific apps

The share sheet works the same way across apps, but the Share button isn't always in the same spot.

**Files app**: Open the PDF, tap the share icon in the lower-left corner, scroll down to Print.

**Mail**: Tap and hold the PDF attachment, tap Share, then Print.

**Safari**: If the PDF opens in the browser, tap the share icon in the toolbar, then Print.

**Photos**: If you saved the PDF to Photos, open it, tap the share icon, then Print.

If Print does not appear in the share sheet, scroll through the actions row, tap the "More" option (sometimes shown as three dots), and enable Print from the list. Some apps bury it.

## Preparing your PDF before printing

A little prep can save paper and time.

**Only need a few pages?** Use the [Split PDF tool](/split-pdf) in iPhone's browser to pull out just the pages you need. Printing a 40-page report when you want 4 pages wastes ink.

**Combining two files?** Use the [Merge PDF tool](/merge-pdf) to join them into one before you print. One print job is easier to manage than two.

**Large image-heavy PDF loading slowly?** The [Compress PDF tool](/compress-pdf) can shrink it. Only use this if your PDF is full of photos or scanned images, not if it is a text document. The compressor rasterizes every page to JPEG, which makes text-only files significantly larger rather than smaller.

All three tools run in Safari on iPhone with no sign-up or app download required.

## How to save content as a PDF (print to PDF)

If someone told you to "print it as a PDF", they mean saving content as a PDF file, not sending it to a printer.

On iPhone, you start the same way as printing:

1. Open the document or web page.
2. Tap Share, then Print.
3. On the print preview screen, place two fingers on the thumbnail and spread them apart (pinch out).
4. The preview expands into a full-screen PDF. Tap the share icon to save it to Files or send it elsewhere.

This works in Safari, Mail, Notes, and most apps that support printing. No app or account needed.

## Why won't my PDF print from iPhone?

A few common causes and their fixes.

**No printers found**: Confirm that your iPhone and printer are on the same WiFi network on the same band. Restart the printer if it still does not appear.

**PDF has a password**: A locked PDF can't be printed without entering the password first. The Files app will prompt for the password when you open it. If you want to remove the lock before printing, use the [Unlock PDF tool](/unlock-pdf) with the correct password.

**Pages are cut off**: The PDF's page size may not match your paper size. In the print dialog, look for a "Fit to Page" or "Scale" toggle and turn it on.

**Pages print blank**: This sometimes happens with scanned PDFs that have a rendering issue. Try opening the file in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free on the App Store) and printing from there instead.

**Print option is greyed out**: Some PDFs have a print restriction set by the creator. The file opens fine but the print command is disabled. The only fix is to remove that restriction, which requires knowing the owner password for the document.
