## How to convert a screenshot to PDF on Windows

Windows 10 and 11 include a built-in PDF printer, so no extra software is needed.

1. Find your screenshot in File Explorer.
2. Right-click it and choose **Open with > Photos**.
3. Click the printer icon at the top-right of the Photos window.
4. In the Printer dropdown, choose **Microsoft Print to PDF**.
5. Click **Print** and choose where to save the file.

You can also press **Ctrl + P** from inside Photos to open the same dialog. The resulting PDF matches the dimensions of the original screenshot.

If Microsoft Print to PDF isn't showing in your printer list, add it through **Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners > Add a printer or scanner**.

## How to convert a screenshot to PDF on Mac

[Preview](https://support.apple.com/guide/preview/welcome/mac) is installed on every Mac and handles this conversion natively.

1. Double-click your screenshot to open it in Preview.
2. Go to **File > Export as PDF**.
3. Name the file, pick a save location, and click **Save**.

For multiple screenshots, select them all in Finder, right-click and choose **Open With > Preview**. All images open together in a single Preview window. Then go to **File > Print**, click the **PDF** dropdown at the bottom-left, and choose **Save as PDF**. Preview produces a single document with each screenshot on its own page.

## How to convert a screenshot to PDF on iPhone

iOS has a built-in Print-to-PDF path that most people don't know about. It's been available since [iOS 11](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111904) and requires no apps.

1. Open your screenshot in the **Photos** app.
2. Tap the **Share** button (box with an arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Print**.
4. On the print preview, place two fingers on the image and spread them apart (pinch out). The image switches to a PDF view.
5. Tap **Share** again and choose **Save to Files**, or send it straight to another app.

One note: this trick creates a single-page PDF. If you need multiple screenshots in one PDF, use the [Images to PDF tool](/jpg-to-pdf) instead, which lets you add several images and download them as one document.

## How to convert a screenshot to PDF on Android

On most Android phones, the print system doubles as a PDF exporter.

1. Open your screenshot in **Google Photos** (or your default gallery app).
2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top-right.
3. Choose **Print**.
4. In the printer selector at the top, tap **Save as PDF**.
5. Tap the PDF icon and choose a save location.

Samsung's Gallery app follows the same flow. If you're on a device where the gallery app doesn't have a Print option, the [Images to PDF tool](/jpg-to-pdf) is the simplest alternative since it works in any mobile browser.

## Will my screenshots lose quality?

The OS-level methods (Windows Print to PDF, Mac Preview, iOS Print to PDF, Android Save as PDF) don't re-encode your image. They wrap the existing file into a PDF container, keeping the original resolution. If a PDF looks blurry on screen, zoom to 100%, because most PDF viewers show pages scaled down by default.

Screen resolution also matters. A screenshot taken on a standard HD display at 1920x1080 produces a sharper PDF than one from a 720p screen, because there are simply more pixels to start with.
