Compressing a PDF for Telegram takes about ten seconds and requires no account. Open the [Compress PDF tool](/compress-pdf), drop in your file, pick a level, and download the result. That said, compression only helps if your PDF is image-heavy or scanned. If it's a text document, compressing it will make it larger, not smaller.

## Why compress a PDF before sending it on Telegram?

[Telegram](https://telegram.org) lets free accounts send files up to 2 GB each, so the hard limit isn't usually the problem. The real issue is upload and download speed, particularly on mobile.

A 40 MB PDF of scanned pages can take a long time to upload on a slow connection, and recipients on mobile data pay for every megabyte they download. Compressing that same file down to 12 MB makes a real difference in how quickly the conversation moves.

The other common situation is Telegram bots. The [Telegram Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#sending-files) caps file uploads at 50 MB. If you're sending a PDF through a bot (for a workflow, a channel bot, or a file management bot), anything over 50 MB will fail, and compression is often the fix.

## When compression helps and when it doesn't

This is the part most compression guides skip, and it matters.

**Compress if your PDF contains:**
- Scanned documents (photos of physical pages)
- Images, photographs, or diagrams that fill most of the page
- A mix of photos and some text

In testing, a six-page image-heavy PDF shrank from 913.4 KB to 315.3 KB at Medium quality, a reduction of 65.5%. At High (smallest), the same file shrank to 175.6 KB.

**Do not compress if your PDF contains:**
- Typed text (reports, invoices, contracts, presentations with mostly text)
- Forms you've filled in
- Documents where you need to preserve selectable, copyable text

A 20-page text report that started at 23.5 KB grew to 1.4 MB after compression. That's not a bug. The tool rasterizes every page to a JPEG, which is efficient for pages that are already photographs and brutal for pages that are vector text. If you try compressing a text PDF and the output is bigger, that's the reason.

## How to compress a PDF for Telegram

1. Go to the [Compress PDF tool](/compress-pdf). No sign-up required.
2. Click "Choose file" or drag your PDF onto the page.
3. Select a compression level. If you're not sure, start with Medium.
4. Click "Compress PDF."
5. Download the compressed file.
6. In Telegram, tap the attachment icon, choose **File** (not Photo/Video), and select the compressed PDF.

Everything runs inside your browser. The PDF isn't sent to any server.

## Which compression level should you use?

Three levels are available:

| Level | Image-deck result | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Low (best quality) | 913.4 KB to 703.5 KB (−23%) | Portfolios, print-ready reports |
| Medium | 913.4 KB to 315.3 KB (−65.5%) | Most Telegram sharing |
| High (smallest) | 913.4 KB to 175.6 KB (−80.8%) | When you need the smallest possible file |

These figures come from a six-page image deck. Results vary depending on what's in your specific file.

Pick the level once and download. **Don't compress the output again.** Running a second pass at the same level caused a file to grow from 315.3 KB to 598.9 KB in testing. The already-rasterized page gets re-encoded and ends up larger. If Medium isn't enough, go back to the original and try High.

## What to do when compression makes the file bigger

If the compressed file comes back larger than the original, your PDF is text-based and rasterization is working against you. You have a few straightforward alternatives.

**Split the PDF.** The [Split PDF tool](/split-pdf) lets you extract page ranges as separate files. A 30-page contract becomes three 10-page PDFs, each small enough to send quickly. Recipients can scroll through them in Telegram's built-in viewer without downloading anything.

**Zip the file.** Text PDFs compress very well inside a ZIP archive. Right-click the file on Windows or macOS and choose "Compress" or "Send to Zip." Telegram accepts ZIP files, and the size reduction for text-heavy content is often 50% or more.

**Send page ranges.** Sometimes just extracting the pages the recipient actually needs is the right move. If someone needs to review pages 5 to 12 of a 40-page document, use the [Split PDF tool](/split-pdf) to pull those pages out.

## How to send a PDF as a document on Telegram

When you attach a file in Telegram, there's a meaningful choice between sending it as a **File** versus from your photo gallery.

- **File / Document**: Telegram sends the PDF as-is, shows the filename, and lets the recipient open it in a PDF viewer. This is what you want.
- **Gallery**: Telegram may display it as an image thumbnail or convert it, depending on the app version and platform.

On mobile, tap the attachment icon (paperclip on Android, the plus sign on iOS), then choose **File** or **Document**, and navigate to the PDF on your device.

## A note on privacy

If the PDF contains sensitive information, contracts, identity documents, or medical records, it's worth knowing how each step handles your data.

The Compress PDF tool runs entirely in your browser. The file never leaves your device during compression, so there's nothing to intercept.

Telegram uses [client-server encryption](https://telegram.org/privacy) for regular chats and end-to-end encryption for Secret Chats. For confidential documents, use a Secret Chat or consider whether Telegram is the right channel at all.
