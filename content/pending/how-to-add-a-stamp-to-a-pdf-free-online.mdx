## What is a PDF stamp?

A PDF stamp is a visible label placed directly on a PDF page. It communicates the document's current status to anyone who opens or prints it. Typical labels are APPROVED, DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, PAID, and VOID, though the text can be anything you choose.

[PDF annotations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDF#Annotations) have been part of the PDF specification since Adobe introduced them, and stamps are one of the oldest and most practical annotation types. They sit on top of the existing page content, which means the original text and graphics underneath stay intact.

You'll see stamps used across many fields. Legal teams mark contracts as DRAFT while they're still being reviewed. Accounting departments stamp invoices as PAID once settled. HR departments label confidential files accordingly. The pattern is consistent: a quick, clear label that tells anyone who opens the file what they're looking at.

### Stamps vs. watermarks: what's the difference?

People use the words interchangeably, and the tools often blur the line too. Here's a practical way to think about it:

- **Stamps** sit on top of the page content, usually opaque, and mark a specific status.
- **Watermarks** appear behind the page content, usually semi-transparent, and repeat across every page for branding or to deter unauthorized copying.

Both are useful. A stamp is what you want when you need a clear, readable status label. A watermark is better when you want something subtle in the background, like a company name or logo that appears on every printed page.

## Common stamp labels and when to use each

| Label | Typical use |
|---|---|
| DRAFT | The document is not final; shared for review or comment. |
| APPROVED | Someone with authority has signed off on this version. |
| CONFIDENTIAL | Contains sensitive information; distribution should be limited. |
| PAID | An invoice or bill has been settled. |
| VOID | The document is no longer valid, such as a superseded check or replaced contract. |
| RECEIVED | The document was physically or digitally received on a given date. |
| COPY | This is not the original; treat it as a reference copy only. |
| REJECTED | The document or submission has been declined. |

You're not limited to these. Many teams stamp documents with a reviewer's initials and a date, or a version number, so they can track which revision is which. Any short phrase works as a stamp.
